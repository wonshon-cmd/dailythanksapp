# Daily Journey Privacy Policy

> For Squarespace: paste into the dailyjourney.org/privacy page.
> Replace every `[bracket]`. Have it reviewed by a privacy professional before launch.

**Effective date: November 21, 2026**

Daily Journey, Inc. ("we") runs the Daily Journey app and website (the "Service"). This policy explains what we collect, why, how we protect it, and the choices you have.

## 1. What we collect

| Category | Data | When |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Email address and your name / if you sign in with Google or Apple, that account’s identifier and email | When you sign in |
| Your content | Notes of thanks, thank-you cards (including the recipient name you type), prayer requests, reflections, visibility setting | When you write |
| Communities | Communities you create or join, your role (admin, leader, member), join requests | When you create or join one |
| Activity | Likes, saves, shares, reports, blocks, hidden posts, posts viewed, dates written, whether you finished today’s passage or a reading-plan day (no text) | When you use the Service |
| Settings | Language, text size, reminder time, features turned on or off | When you change them |
| Device | App version | When you open the app |

- Reading reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification token is sent to us.
- Your own notes and highlights are locked on your device and are not sent to our servers.

- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.
- We do **not** collect location.
- We do **not** use your data for advertising.
- The Service is not for children under 14. In the U.S., it is not directed to children under 13.

## 2. Why we use it

1. Create and keep your account signed in
2. Store your notes and show them only to the audience you choose (Only me, a group, or Everyone)
3. Send one reading reminder at your chosen time (skipped on days you’ve already read)
4. Keep the community safe: filtering abusive language, handling reports, blocking and hiding
5. Fix bugs and improve the Service

## 3. How long we keep it

- Your account and notes are **deleted when you delete your account**.
- Report records are kept for one year after resolution, for safety and disputes. The reporter is anonymized when their account is deleted.
- We keep data longer only where the law requires it.

## 4. Sharing

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not share your personal information with third parties, except our service providers below or where required by law.

## 5. Service providers (may process data outside your country)

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase, Inc. | Sign-in, storage |
| Google LLC · Apple Inc. (optional) | Confirming who you are if you sign in with them |
| Email provider used by Supabase | Sending sign-in codes by email |

Data is encrypted in transit.

## 6. Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data, or ask us to stop processing it:

- **In the app:** Settings › About › Delete account
- **On the web:** dailyjourney.org/delete-account
- **By email:** hi@dailyjourney.org

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have additional rights, including data portability and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. We honor these rights for all users.

## 7. Security

We protect your data with:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
- **Only-me notes:** private notes are locked (encrypted) on your device and are not sent to our servers. The key stays on your device, so not even we can read them.
- **Posts you share with a community or with everyone:** stored on our servers (Supabase). Database access rules let only that community’s members (or signed-in users) read them. They are not end-to-end encrypted, so we can read them to run the service and handle reports.
- Other people’s group posts can’t be shared or copied in the app, and screenshots are blocked on Android (on iPhone the screen is hidden while it’s being recorded).
- Server-side access rules, so no one else can read your private notes
- Access logging
- Least-privilege access for operators

## 8. Changes

We will post changes in the app or on our website at least 7 days before they take effect, and 30 days before for significant changes.
